# 🚶‍♂️ SafeStreet

SafeStreet is a web-based prototype designed to help people understand how safe and walkable a route is before choosing to walk.  
Instead of focusing only on distance or time, SafeStreet emphasizes **pedestrian safety, walkability, and people-first urban design**.

This project was built as part of a hackathon to explore how digital tools can support safer, healthier, and more sustainable cities.

---

## 🌍 Problem Statement
Many cities are designed primarily for vehicles rather than pedestrians.  
As a result, people face unsafe walking conditions due to:
- Poor or broken footpaths
- Inadequate street lighting
- Unsafe pedestrian crossings
- High traffic density

This discourages walking, increases pollution and transport costs, and negatively affects public health and quality of life.

---

## 🎯 Solution
SafeStreet acts as a **Walking Decision Assistant**.

Users enter a **starting location** and **destination**, and the system provides:
- A **Walkability Score (0–100)**
- A clear **safety verdict** (Safe / Caution / Avoid)
- A transparent **risk breakdown** explaining *why* the verdict was given

The goal is not just to show data, but to help users make **informed walking decisions**.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- Simple input: From → To location
- Walkability score with color-coded feedback
- Clear safety verdict:
  - 🟢 Safe to Walk
  - 🟡 Walk with Caution
  - 🔴 Avoid Walking
- Risk breakdown based on:
  - Traffic conditions
  - Lighting
  - Footpath quality
  - Pedestrian crossings
- Clean, responsive, and demo-friendly UI

> ⚠️ Note: This is a prototype. The scoring logic is simulated for validation and demonstration purposes.
